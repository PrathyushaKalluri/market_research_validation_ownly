## 1. Headline answers

1. **Target restaurants, stated:** local and regional independents that the incumbents underserve. Sanka: "Swiggy and Zomato are built only for nationalised chains, or brands. Not for the local/regional ones." **COMPANY CLAIM** (S18, Startuppedia / Moneycontrol interview, 2026-07-06). Inc42 describes the pool as ~70,000 unlisted Bengaluru FSSAI restaurants: thaali joints, dhabas, tiffin centres. **MEDIA REPORT** (S10).
2. **Who is actually on it:** a mix, not only small eateries. Named partners include:
   - Bengaluru heritage and mid-price chains: Empire, Vidyarthi Bhavan, Amintiri (S20)
   - Hyderabad regional chains: Paradise Biryani, Bawarchi, Cream Stone and others (S32)
   - National brands at pilot, via aggregator reports: Eatfit, Wow! Momo, Faasos, Krispy Kreme (S07)
   - KFC and McDonald's, listed through magicpin and then taken offline in May 2026 (S17)

   Labels: **COMPANY CLAIM** and **MEDIA REPORT**.
3. **Restaurant-side model:** in 2026 Ownly says restaurants pay nothing: "no commissions… no marketing fees… no subscription fees" (S13, FPJ, 2026-03-04). **COMPANY CLAIM**. The model has changed and reports conflict:
   - Pilot (2025): flat Rs 25/Rs 50 per order, restaurants bearing delivery within 4 km (S01–S04, S08)
   - Business Standard, July 2026: still says "subscription fee" (S16)
4. **Scale claims:** ~20,000 restaurants (Mar/Jul/Aug 2026), 22,000 (website), ~25,000 (Jul/Aug 2026). ~50,000 daily orders and ~10% of Bengaluru orders (Aug 2026). All **COMPANY CLAIM** or **MEDIA REPORT** from one Moneycontrol upstream (S12, S24, S25, S31).
5. **Target customers, stated:** price-sensitive diners, especially Rapido riders who have **never ordered food online**. The goal is to expand the market to 100 million online food orderers, not to steal existing users. **COMPANY CLAIM** (S37, S39, S40). Ownly gives no age or occupation target in any company statement found.
6. **Price positioning:** menu prices equal to offline, "Ownly pay for Food + Delivery" (S41), ~15% cheaper than incumbents (S06). At pilot, a "Meals for One" section with ≥4 meals under ₹150 (S05, S38). An "AOV ~60% of industry average" target appears only in an unsourced Whalesbook piece (S46). **COMPANY CLAIM** and **MEDIA REPORT**.
7. **Students and young people:** the only analyst-type source is a JP Morgan note (via Storyboard18, 2026-06-16). It describes the *budget segment* (Toing) as "students and younger users" and says Toing was "materially more successful" than Ownly. No metrics were disclosed. **MEDIA REPORT** (S49). Zomato calls both apps "specifically targeting budget-conscious customers" (S51).
8. **Hyderabad:** the only evidence is Ownly's own landing page (S32, S33, S57) and Facebook page (S59). It names 8 brands and shows ₹249 vs ₹420 style price comparisons. No launch date, localities, restaurant count or offers are given, and no independent news confirmation was found. **COMPANY CLAIM**.

### 2b. Restaurant segment profile

**Evidenced**

- **Size and type: mixed.**
  - Stated priority: local and regional independents not yet online (S18, S10).
  - Named partners, however, are mostly well-known multi-outlet regional brands:
    - Empire and Vidyarthi Bhavan in Bengaluru
    - Paradise, Bawarchi, Cream Stone and 100N in Hyderabad
  - National chains and cloud kitchens (Eatfit, Faasos, Wow! Momo, Krispy Kreme) were reported at pilot (S07).
  - KFC and McDonald's came via magicpin and were then taken offline (S17).
- **Price tier:** built around everyday and budget meals. "Meals for One", ≥4 meals under ₹150, and staples under ₹100 at pilot (S05, S38). Offline-equal menu pricing (S02, S18).
- **Commercial model:**
  - 2025 pilot: flat per-order fee Rs 25/50, restaurants bearing delivery within 4 km (S01–S04, S08).
  - 2026 company position: zero charges to restaurants, customer pays ~₹30 + GST delivery (S13, S22, S26, S56).
- **Onboarding channels:**
  - Mainly a direct merchant team (S09)
  - NRAI: non-exclusive partnership 2025, MoU July 2026 (S01, S20)
  - magicpin: small share (S09, S17)
- **Owner voices:**
  - Positive: Empire's order ramp (S23)
  - Cautious: trust and data-terms worries (S28); associations "evaluating" Ownly as a boycott alternative (S27)

**INTERPRETATION**

- The "small local eatery" story is the pitch. The named showcase partners suggest that popular regional brands, whose menus already sell at offline prices, are the anchor supply that drives order volume. A likely tactic: flagship brands pull in demand, the long tail fills out selection.
- Removing fees mainly benefits low-ticket restaurants, where a 25–30% commission is most painful. That fits the Meals-for-One and under-₹150 rules.

**UNKNOWN**

- Split of restaurants by size, chain vs independent, cloud kitchen, or price tier (no data published)
- Whether Domino's, Burger King or Pizza Hut are listed, or whether KFC and McDonald's came back
- Any formal BBHA partnership (none found; BBHA only appears in boycott context) or Petpooja/POS integration (none found)
- Current menu-parity enforcement, minimum order value (App Store says "No small order fee"), and whether the 4 km radius still applies
- Public restaurant terms (ownly.food/restaurant-terms did not render)

### 3b. Customer segment profile

**Evidenced (company statements)**

- **Primary target:** price-sensitive consumers for whom total cost is the barrier. Especially the large Rapido rider base (S55) who have never ordered food online (S37, S39, S40).
- **Offer:** offline-equal menu prices, pay only for food plus delivery (S41, S42). Everyday low prices, not coupons (S44). Low-ticket single meals, ₹100–150 (S38).
- **Geography:** Bengaluru pilot in Koramangala, HSR and BTM (S47). Citywide March 2026. Stated ambition covers metros and Tier 2/3 (S43).

**MEDIA REPORT (not company-stated)**

- JP Morgan groups the budget segment as students and younger users, and says Toing leads it (S49).

**INTERPRETATION**

- "Meals for One", under-₹150 meals, meme-style Instagram content about Bengaluru life (S41) and the Koramangala/HSR/BTM pilot all point in practice to young singles: working professionals and students in rented or PG housing.
- Koramangala, HSR and BTM are widely known as dense young-professional residential hubs. **No source says this is why they were chosen.**
- The company's *stated* segment is broader: first-time online orderers, including Tier 2/3.

**UNKNOWN**

- Age, occupation, income or household mix of actual Ownly users. No survey, filing or app-analytics dataset was found.
- Ownly's actual AOV, repeat-rate figures, and the share of orders from Rapido riders.
- Launch offers or referral schemes. None found; the Hyderabad page only offers "exclusive offers and early access" via email signup.
- Consumer discussion on Reddit. The site links r/ownlyfoodapp, but no threads surfaced in search.

## 5. Implications for the Gachibowli study (INTERPRETATION)

**Competitor audit sample (verify availability in-app before fieldwork)**

1. **Showcase regional chains from ownly.food/hyd**, where they have outlets serving Gachibowli: Paradise, Bawarchi, Cream Stone, 100N, Vivaha Bhojanambu, Punjabi Affair, Harley's, Bakelore. These test the company's own price-parity claims directly.
2. **Independent budget eateries and tiffin/meals places near tech parks and PG clusters.** This is the stated core target (S10, S18) and the under-₹150 "Meals for One" tier (S05).
3. **National QSR and cloud-kitchen brands** (KFC, McDonald's, Domino's, Burger King, Faasos/EatSure, Wow! Momo). Record presence or absence, given the Bengaluru delisting (S17).
4. For each item, compare the same dish and outlet across Ownly, Swiggy and Zomato at the same time. Record:
   - Menu price, delivery fee, platform/packaging fees, final payable
   - Delivery ETA, "Meals for One" availability, and whether the outlet is serviceable at ~4 km

**Does the company's target match the 20–30 students/professionals focus?**

- **Partly.** The price point (₹100–250 single meals), marketing tone and Bengaluru pilot areas fit young singles. JP Morgan also frames the budget segment as students and younger users (S49).
- **But** Ownly's stated growth thesis is *first-time* online orderers from Rapido's rider base (S37, S39). Most Gachibowli 20–30 professionals already use Swiggy or Zomato heavily. For Ownly they are a *switching* segment, not the *market-expansion* segment it emphasises.
- **Survey should capture:**
  - Rapido usage frequency
  - Current delivery-app usage and spend per order
  - Whether respondents order for one or for a group
  - Sensitivity to fees vs menu price
  - Whether their usual restaurants appear on Ownly

  This tests which of the two segments our sample represents.

## 6. Contradictions and gaps

1. **Restaurant fees.** Four versions over time, some overlapping:
   - Rs 25/50 per order (Aug–Nov 2025: S02, S08)
   - "absolutely no charges" (Mar 2026 onward: S13, S22)
   - "subscription fee" (Business Standard Jul 2026: S16; Whalesbook: S19)
   - Entrackr's "Rapido: 8–15%" figure (S03)

   The current written merchant terms are UNKNOWN.
2. **Who pays delivery.**
   - Restaurants within 4 km (2025: S01, S04)
   - Waived for customers during pilot (S30); Inc42 Nov 2025 says no customer delivery fee (S08)
   - ~₹30 + GST paid by customers, flat or per-km (2026: S26, S56)
   - Yet the App Store still says "No delivery fee" (S52)
3. **Restaurant counts:** 2,300 (Inc42 feature, date unclear) vs ~20,000 vs 22,000 vs ~25,000 around the same weeks. NRAI membership is quoted as 50,000 (S01) and as 500,000 (OfficeChai).
4. **Launch dates and pilot areas:** citywide "February" (OfficeChai) vs 3 March 2026. Pilot described as two areas (Koramangala, HSR) or three (plus BTM).
5. **Chains:** Sanka says incumbents serve chains while Ownly serves local eateries. Yet national chains and cloud kitchens were listed, and regional chains are the showcase partners.
6. **Traction vs Toing:** JP Morgan (June 2026) says Toing is ahead, but gives no metrics. Moneycontrol-sourced reports (Aug 2026) show Ownly at 50k orders/day. Different periods, no common metric.
7. **Price gap:** "~15% cheaper" (S06) vs 19–41% examples on the Hyderabad page (S33).
8. **Independence:** the NRAI MoU coverage is one press release; the 50k-orders stories are one Moneycontrol report; the Sanka "100 million" quotes are one interview. Treat each cluster as a single source.

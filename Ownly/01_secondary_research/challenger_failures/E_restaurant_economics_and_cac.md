# A) COMMISSION RATES CHARGED TO RESTAURANTS

## A.1 The regulatory record (strongest evidence)

**FACT — CCI Section 26(1) order, 4 April 2022, Case No. 16 of 2021 (NRAI v. Zomato/Swiggy).**
Confirmed verbatim in **Swiggy's own DRHP** (a SEBI filing), page 385:

> "The CCI through an order dated **April 4, 2022**, directed the Director General ("DG") to investigate in accordance with section 26(1) of the Competition Act, whether the conduct of our Company with respect to, inter alia, **(a) exclusivity with restaurant partners (RPs); (b) requirement to maintain list price parity of dishes offered by RPs on channels of third parties; and (c) presence of our Company in downstream market adversely impacts the competition between the Restaurant Partners and the private brands operated by our Company**, thereby hampering the neutrality of our Company as a platform; had resulted in a contravention of the provisions of section 3(1) read with section 3(4) of the Competition Act."
> — Swiggy Limited DRHP, filed Sept 2024. https://business.bofa.com/content/dam/flagship/in/en/investment-banking/Swiggy-Limited-DRHP.pdf

**Critically: the CCI's 26(1) order did NOT admit the commission-rate allegation for investigation.** AZB & Partners' case note states the Commission found price parity, exclusivity and private-label conflict warranted investigation, while "**other allegations were deemed not to directly impact competition**" — i.e. commission rates, bundled services, one-sided contracts and deep discounting were *not* taken up at that stage.
— AZB & Partners, undated case note. https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/orders-under-section-261-of-the-act-cci-directs-investigation-against-zomato-and-swiggy/ — **FACT (regulatory summary)**

**FACT — DG report status, per Swiggy DRHP:**
> "The DG has submitted its investigation report to the CCI and the CCI has made a copy of the non-confidential version of such report available to our Company. Separately, our Company had filed a writ petition dated **May 17, 2024** before the High Court of Karnataka at Bengaluru, challenging an order dated **April 24, 2024** issued by the CCI which allowed the Opposite Parties to create a confidentiality ring… Through order dated **June 26, 2024**, the Karnataka High Court set aside the Impugned Order and remitted the matter back to the CCI for reconsideration."

## A.2 The leaked DG findings (Nov 2024)

**MEDIA REPORT — Reuters exclusive, 8 November 2024**, reported via MediaNama and others. The DG report was shared with the parties in **March 2024**; findings were never made public.

Key findings as reported:
- Zomato entered **"exclusivity contracts" with restaurant partners in return for lower commissions**; Swiggy promised business growth to players listing exclusively.
- CCI's investigation arm: exclusivity arrangements **"prevent the market from becoming more competitive."**
- Zomato imposed pricing/discount restrictions **with penal provisions for non-compliance**; Swiggy warned partners of **rank downgrades** if they offered lower prices elsewhere.
- Zomato's defence: **"around 1% of all restaurant partners listed on Zomato were exclusive to it"** (as of 2021).
- Data masking: platforms withheld consumer data from restaurants while holding them accountable for order issues.
— MediaNama, 8 Nov 2024. https://www.medianama.com/2024/11/223-cci-finds-zomato-swiggy-violating-competition-laws-report/
— Business Standard, 8 Nov 2024. https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/cci-probe-finds-zomato-swiggy-breached-antitrust-regulations-report-124110800886_1.html

**COMPANY CLAIM (rebuttal), Nov 2024:** Both companies stated no final order has been passed and this is a preliminary investigative stage.
— YourStory, Nov 2024. https://yourstory.com/2024/11/awaiting-final-decision-cci-antitrust-zomato-swiggy

## A.5 The one thing platforms *have* confirmed about commission direction

**FACT — Eternal (Zomato) Q2FY26 Shareholders' Letter, quarter ending 30 Sep 2025.** CFO Akshant Goyal, answering why food-delivery take rate rose ~75bps QoQ:

> "The take rate increased by about 75 bps whereas the increase in Contribution margin was only about 50 bps. **The increase in take rate was driven by improvements across multiple levers including ad monetization, commission rates and increase in platform fee.** This increase was partly offset by the decrease in delivery charges as we lowered the minimum order value for free delivery as part of our Gold program to INR 99 from INR 199."

https://b.zmtcdn.com/investor-relations/Eternal_Shareholders_Letter_Q2FY26_Results.pdf — **FACT.** This is the only primary-source confirmation I found that **commission rates were actively increased** in this period.

## B.2 The critical disclosure break: GOV is no longer reported

**FACT — Eternal Q1FY26 Shareholders' Letter (quarter ending 30 June 2025):**
> "In response to the sluggish demand environment, we saw a **QoQ increase in restaurant funded discounts (as a % of GOV)** in Q1FY26, which led to the slightly lower NOV growth vis-à-vis GOV growth during the quarter (**NOV = GOV minus discounts**). We expect such quarterly fluctuations to be a regular feature as restaurants calibrate their investments in discounts in response to changes in the demand environment."
>
> "Akshant: **Going forward, we will only be disclosing NOV (and will discontinue disclosing GOV)**, as we believe that NOV is a better measure of growth in our B2C businesses including food delivery."

https://b.zmtcdn.com/investor-relations/Eternal_Shareholders_Letter_Q1FY26_Results.pdf

Confirmed again in **Q1FY27 (22 Jul 2026)**: *"GOV YoY growth was a few percentage points higher (sunsetting this metric disclosure from this quarter onwards…)"*

**→ Research implication (important for your project):** Eternal has **removed the only public metric from which restaurant-funded discounts could be inferred.** The GOV–NOV gap was the observable proxy for restaurant discount co-funding; it is now permanently unobservable. This is a **FACT** and a defensible analytical point.

## F.4 Discounting — what is actually disclosed

- **Eternal:** restaurant-funded discounts are acknowledged as a real, fluctuating line (**FACT**, Q1FY26 letter) but **the magnitude was never disclosed and GOV reporting has now been sunset**, removing even the inferential route.

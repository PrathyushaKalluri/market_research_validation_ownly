## Standing caveats

- **This is secondary research.** Per `_ops/direction.md` it does not outrank first-party Hyderabad
  evidence, and it decides nothing. It generates predictions (D §6); the survey, fake door,
  interviews and audit test them.
- Every claim carries a source and an evidence label (FACT / MEDIA REPORT / COMPANY CLAIM /
  ANALYST ESTIMATE / CONTESTED / UNKNOWN). Where sources conflict, both are shown.
- **52 explicit UNKNOWNs in C alone.** Nothing was estimated to fill a gap. In particular
  **Ownly's burn per order, CAC and CLV are UNKNOWN** and must not be quoted from any source.
- Six failures is a pattern, not a law. D §7 states what this evidence cannot establish.

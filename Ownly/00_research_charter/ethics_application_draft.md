## 1. Purpose

We want to understand how people aged 20–30 around Gachibowli order food delivery. We are interested in:
- what frustrates them (fees, delays, cancellations)
- what would make them switch apps
- whether a lower-fee proposition, like that of Rapido's Ownly service, fits their needs

This is independent student research. We have no affiliation with, or funding from, Ownly, Rapido, Swiggy, Zomato or any other company.

## 3. Recruitment
- Survey link shared in student and residential (PG/co-living) WhatsApp/Telegram groups **with group admin permission**, on team members' LinkedIn, and by friends forwarding it.
- No cold messages to strangers. No pressure; participation is voluntary.
- Interview participants are invited individually and can decline without giving a reason.

## 4. Informed consent
- **Survey:** the first page explains the purpose, the team's independence, time needed, anonymity, voluntary participation, the right to stop at any time, data use and deletion, and a contact email. Respondents must tick "Yes, I agree" to continue.
- **Interviews:** the same information is read aloud, including the purpose of any recording. Verbal consent is recorded in the notes before starting. Participants can skip questions or stop at any time. Script: `04_interviews/consent_and_privacy.md`.

## 5. Risks and mitigation
- **Risk level:** minimal. Everyday consumer topics, no sensitive categories (health, religion, politics, finances beyond approximate food spend).
- **Discomfort:** all open-text questions are optional; participants can stop at any time.
- **Accidental identification:** no identifiers are collected; small groups (fewer than 10 people) will not be reported separately.
- **Hypothetical app prices** are clearly labelled "made-up examples, not real bills from any app".

## 6. Data storage and deletion
- Survey responses are stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to the 3 team members.
- Interview notes are de-identified. Recordings, if any, are deleted within 48 hours of note-taking.
- All raw data will be deleted after the course grade is released. Only aggregated results appear in the report.

# Dashboard Storyline (viva / deck order)

**One-line thesis to test (not to assert):** *Ownly's Bengaluru playbook can be transferred to Gachibowli, but price
probably wins the first order while supply and reliability decide the second.*

| Beat | Page | The question the audience has | What we show | The sentence we're allowed to say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | "So, does it transfer?" | H₀ banner + overall call + guardrail cards | Only what the decision rules output, with its confidence label |
| 2 | 2 | "What is the playbook, exactly?" | Timeline, fee model by period, sourced scale | "Reported by [source, date]", never "Ownly achieved" |
| 3 | 3 | "Who in Hyderabad are we talking about?" | Profile, frequency, funnel, trade-offs | Sample statements with n |
| 4 | 4 | "What gets the first order, and what the second?" | Paired drivers, repeat by failure | ✓/✕ on each of the 3 hypothesis conditions |
| 5 | 5 | "Is it really cheaper?" | Waterfall, per-basket savings, ETA trade-off | "In k of n matched baskets at one Gachibowli drop point…" |
| 6 | 6 | "Can it deliver?" | Coverage grid, failure incidence, review themes (caveated) | Reviews = themes to check, not rates |
| 7 | 7 | "Does Rapido give it an unfair advantage?" | Users vs non-users gap, discovery source | "Associated with", never "causes" |
| 8 | 8 | "Are the offers buying loyal users?" | Offer vs organic repeat, promo gap | KEEP / MODIFY / TARGET / DEPRIORITIZE per rule 2.4 |
| 9 | 9 | "Where should Hyderabad start?" | Segment matrix, raw values | "Highest on X and Y, lowest on Z", no invented score |
| 10 | 10 | "So what should Ownly do?" | KEEP/ADAPT/DROP cards, H₀ means / doesn't mean, next validation | The call + what would change it |

**Honesty slide (always included):** not calculable (CAC, CLV, GMV, burn/order); sampling limits; which KPIs are stated
vs observed; the fake door's status.

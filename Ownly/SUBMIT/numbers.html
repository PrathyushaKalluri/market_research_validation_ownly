Every number, and exactly how it was calculated.

Each entry gives the figure, the formula in words, what was on the top and bottom of the fraction, which file it comes from, and what it cannot be used to claim.

The one rule that governs this whole study: if a figure could not be computed from evidence actually held, it is marked NOT COMPUTABLE with a reason. Nothing is estimated, imputed or filled in to look complete.

All confidence intervals are Wilson score intervals at 95%. They are reported on every proportion because at n = 40 a point estimate alone would overstate what the sample supports.

The ₹30 trade-off — the study's central result

Design: each respondent chose between ₹260 and ₹230 three times. The ₹30 saving never changed; only the thing sacrificed changed. Same people, all three times — which is why the significance test is a paired one.

McNemar exact — restaurants vs speedp = 1.5×10⁻⁶
data/tradeoff_tests.csv
What it doesIgnores everyone who answered both scenarios the same way. Looks only at the people who switched, and asks whether switching went one way more than chance allows.
Numbers24 accepted the delay but refused to lose restaurants; 1 did the reverse. Two-sided exact binomial on 25 discordant pairs.
Plain wordsA 24-to-1 split happens by chance about once in 670,000 times.
Why this testThe same 40 people answered both. An unpaired test (chi-square, two-proportion z) would ignore that pairing and be wrong here.
NoteThe related reliability-vs-speed test (p = 0.021, 9 vs 1) is the weakest of the three and would not survive a strict multiple-comparison correction.

What could not be computed, and why

Nine KPIs and ten standard marketing metrics are marked NOT ESTIMABLE. Every one requires Ownly's or Rapido's internal data.

D30 Retained Order Rate — the north starNOT COMPUTABLE
NS · needs company data
Would beD30 repeat rate (%) × average 30-day order frequency among retained users
NeedsPer-user transaction history: first-order date and subsequent order dates, per user.
Why no substituteNo survey question can stand in. Stated intent is not behaviour. K18 (25.0%) is reported as the closest proxy and labelled as such.
Contribution margin per order — the guardrailNOT COMPUTABLE
K23 · needs company data
Why it mattersUber Eats India lost about $2.55 per order against a $2.45 average order value — it lost more per order than the order was worth. Any repeat rate is meaningless without this.
What we did observeOwnly charged ₹0 delivery, ₹0 platform and ₹0 packaging while taking 0% commission, so observed revenue per order in Gachibowli was ₹0 — an observation about the customer's bill, not a claim about Ownly's accounts.
CAC · CLV · Retention rate · Market share · BDI/CDI · EBITDA · CAGRNOT ESTIMABLE
data/not_estimable.csv · formula sheets 1 & 2
Common reasonAll require internal transaction, spend or cost data, or a credible market-size figure. No such figure exists publicly for Gachibowli food delivery, and a 40-person convenience sample cannot stand in for a market.
Where they would come fromOwnly / Rapido internal data, or a commissioned market study.
The findingThat the metrics determining whether Ownly survives are precisely the ones no external researcher can see is itself a material result about the evaluability of this business model.
Preferred-restaurant coverageINSTRUMENT GAP
K6 · our own survey's limitation
Would berespondents' own most-ordered restaurants available on Ownly ÷ their top restaurants
Why not computedThe live instrument never asks respondents to name their usual restaurants. This is our own design gap, not a data-access problem.
The fixOne extra question: "name the 3 places you order from most", then check each on Ownly. Given that 65% will not trade their usual restaurants, this is the version of coverage that would actually predict behaviour.

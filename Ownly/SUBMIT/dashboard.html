01 · Business problem

A cheap first order is easy to buy. A habit is not.

Ownly is a food-delivery app backed by the bike-taxi company Rapido. It charges restaurants no commission and passes the saving on, so the customer's bill is lower. It launched in Bengaluru and is now live in Hyderabad.

Every food-delivery challenger in the Indian record — Foodpanda, Uber Eats, Amazon Food — bought its first orders with discounts and disappeared when the discounts stopped. Foodpanda fell from roughly 200,000 daily orders to about 5,000 once it turned the money off. No challenger in the Indian record has demonstrated post-subsidy retention.

The research question

How large and durable must Ownly's price advantage be to break Swiggy/Zomato lock-in in Gachibowli, and what restaurant coverage, discovery and delivery experience are required so that price-led trial becomes repeat usage?

The decision this serves is per-tactic — KEEP / ADAPT / DEPRIORITISE on the Bengaluru playbook — not whether to enter. Ownly is already live at the audited address.

04 · Analysis

Four findings, each triangulated across more than one kind of evidence.

Finding 1 · Assortment is the binding constraint — not price, speed or reliability

₹30 held constant across three paired choices with the same 40 respondents. Only the thing given up changed, so any difference is attributable to that thing.

Source · data/tradeoff_summary.csv · n = 40 · 95% Wilson CIs 80.1–97.4 · 57.2–83.9 · 22.1–50.5. Confirmed by McNemar exact: assortment vs speed p = 1.5 × 10⁻⁶ (24 switched one way, 1 the other). Triangulation · matches the audit (88.9% catalogue overlap) and the YouTube record ("the number of restaurant need to improve"). Falsified our own pre-registered prediction P3, that the ETA deficit would block adoption.

Finding 2 · The price advantage is real, structural — and erodes to nothing at a real checkout

Share of person × restaurant pairs where Ownly's observed saving clears what that person said they need, recomputed at three different checkouts.

Source · data/switch_threshold_coverage.csv · 132 pairs (33 respondents naming a figure × 4 baskets). 4 said no amount would move them, 3 didn't know — held out, not coded as zero. The structural half · Ownly's fee load is 4.8% against Zomato 15.1% and Swiggy 27.4% on zero-discount captures. Unlike a discount, not charging a fee needs no funding. The vulnerable half · the headline comparison is falsifiable by any coupon, on any day.

Finding 3 · Reliability is accepted at the point of choice and fatal after repetition

Share of coded items mentioning each theme. This is what the conversation is about — not a failure rate.

General discussion · 264 coded of 420 First-hand Ownly users · 97 coded of 123

Source · data/review_theme_summary.csv. App-store reviews sit higher still: fulfilment failure 72.4% and support/refunds 79.3% of 29 coded reviews of 37. The reconciliation · 72.5% accept a reliability risk when choosing (K21), but interviews report people leave an app after repeated bad orders. Different decision moments, both true — so unreliability blocks the habit, not the trial. Caveat · self-selected public posts overstate failure; the comparison between columns carries the information, not either column alone.

06 · Propositions

The Bengaluru playbook, tactic by tactic.

Ownly has escaped the mechanism that killed Foodpanda and Uber Eats India — its saving is structural, not rented. But it has inherited two others: it attacks the side of the market that was never scarce, and it offers no new use case.

Bengaluru tacticCallEvidence behind the call
Charge no platform, packaging or surge feeKeepFee load 5.2% vs incumbent median 23.4% (K3). Structural — survives without subsidy, and no rival can copy it without giving up revenue.
"The total you see is the total you pay"KeepCheapest in 4 of 4 baskets (K1a); median saving ₹114.50 (K2) against a stated bar of ₹30 (K13).
Headline "cheaper than Swiggy & Zomato"Adapt82.5% hold a membership (K11); coverage falls 81% → 56% → 27% (MM1) and after a coupon Ownly is ₹28 dearer. Reframe on the everyday bill.

Rely on Rapido placement for growthAdapt15.2% discovery among those answering (K9); an 84.8% gap (MM8). Interviews point at referral and creators, not placement.
₹100 off your first orderDeprioritisePost-offer repeat intent 25.0% (K18); trial-to-repeat gap 52.5 pp (MM9); 87.5% expect prices to rise (K19). This is rented demand.

  • Transparent everyday pricingThe observed saving clears the stated switching bar of every respondent who named one.

If you remember one sentence

Ownly's price advantage is real and durable, but it is aimed at the one thing this market will not trade — and it is invisible to the 82.5% who already pay for a membership. Fix the restaurant list before spending another rupee on the discount.

07 · Limits

What we would not claim.

A study is only as good as the things it refuses to overstate.

Someone might read this as…What is actually true
"Ownly is 30% cheaper."In 4 matched baskets, at one address, in one dinner slot. Directional evidence, not a market-wide price index.
"82.5% of Hyderabad holds a membership."82.5% of our 40 catchment respondents, who skew student. The 95% interval runs 68–91%.
"Only 5% have tried it."2 people of 40, in a 4-week window that mostly predates Ownly's Hyderabad rollout. Interval 1.4–16.5%. Carries almost no information.
"Half of Ownly orders fail."Never claimed. 49.5% is the share of coded social posts by first-hand users mentioning a failure. Bad orders get posted far more than good ones.
"The Bengaluru pitch doesn't work."We could not detect a difference (p = 0.265, n = 29). Absence of evidence, not evidence of absence — which is exactly what the fake door exists to settle.
"Ownly's delivery is slow because of its riders."A hypothesis. The +17 min gap is flat across all four restaurants, which points at supply density — but the audit did not record outlet distances, so the mechanism is inferred, not shown.
"We interviewed N people."One transcript is documented; the team reported more without logging them. The count is UNKNOWN and no interview finding carries a prevalence figure.

01The problem

Ownly won Bengaluru on the restaurant side. Hyderabad is a different fight.

Why we picked this problem

Ownly (Rapido's food app) charges restaurants no commission and customers no platform fee. In Bengaluru it reached 50,000+ (~10% share) daily orders within a year. It is now running the same playbook in Hyderabad.

Ownly is already live in Gachibowli, so the question is not whether to enter. It is which parts of the Bengaluru playbook still work here, and what has to change.

Problem statement: will Ownly's Bengaluru value proposition transfer to Gachibowli, and what do 20-35 year-old students and professionals there need before they switch and stay?

What happened in Bengaluru

  1. Aug 2025Pilot in 3 Bengaluru areas
  2. Mar 2026Citywide launch, ~20,000 restaurants, zero commission
  3. Jul 20261,000+ restaurants threaten to quit Swiggy/Zomato
  4. Jul 2026Ownly goes live inside the Rapido app
  5. Aug 2026MoU with NRAI; 50,000+ orders a day
  6. Sep 2026Ownly live in Gachibowli, Hyderabad (no restaurant revolt)

We tracked a funnel and the four things that block it. People have heard of Ownly, but hardly anyone orders.

Funnel KPIs: awareness, browse, trial

Bengaluru is only 16 people, so treat its bars as direction, not size.

Barrier KPIs: why the funnel narrows

Line under each bar = 95% interval. Rapido users were less aware of Ownly than non-users (8 of 24 vs 9 of 16).

Why these KPIs: the problem is whether a proven playbook transfers. The funnel shows whether it is working; the barriers show what would stop it working here: rival memberships, belief in the price, staying after the offer, and whether Rapido actually brings people in.

06Analysis 2 - customers

People will trade speed and reliability for ₹30, but not their restaurants. In the fake door, a missing place lost the order about half the time.

Survey: would you take ₹30 off to give this up?

Accepted the wait but refused to lose restaurants (24) vs the reverse (1); McNemar exact p = 1.5e-06.

Fake door: what happened when it wasn't there

"Lost" = went back to their usual app, or didn't order. When people chose a restaurant, 62.5% said it was because it's always good, they make it best or they always order there. Only 12.5% said price.

What gets people to try is different: price (8 of 23) and a refund promise (6 of 23) came top. Their usual restaurants being on Ownly was named by only 2 of 23. Price opens the door; missing restaurants close it.

Evidence: T1, T2, T3, T4, F4-not_he, F4-no_suc, F3-Trust, T5 - see the last page for each calculation.

07Analysis 3 - trust

The problem gets louder the closer people get to a real order. People don't trust the price, or the order, to hold.

What people mention, by who is talking

Share of posts mentioning each theme. This is not a failure rate: people who complain post more. App reviews are 29 coded posts (small). 70.3% of all reviews are 1-star.

Fake door: why people picked their offer

52.9% picked cash now because they may not come back or don't trust later offers (18 of 34). Only 35.3% took Ownly Money for the next order, even though it was worth more.

Interviews: people leave after repeated bad orders, not one. And "what if they increase prices later?"

09Propositions

Don't buy the first order. Earn the second one.

KEEPZero commission, and no platform, packaging or surge fees. Fees are 4.8% of an Ownly bill vs 15.1%-27.4% on rivals. It needs no subsidy, so it cannot run out.
ADAPTStop saying "cheaper than Swiggy/Zomato". Show people their own bill. After a rival coupon the claim holds for only 27.3% of cases, and 82.5% hold a membership.
ADAPTGo deep on local restaurants, not wide. Sign the places people refuse to give up. Only 35% would give up their restaurants; a missing place lost 53.3% of fake-door orders.
ADDRapido Link: paid fast delivery by a nearby captain, with no passenger on board. 45.5% paid ₹15-49 for it; the top worry was a passenger-first detour (11 of 34). Replaces "don't spend on speed".
ADDSame-day refund cover as a ₹9-39 option. Refund failure is in 79.3% of app reviews; "refund if it goes wrong" was the top payment reason.
DEPRIORITISEBig first-order discounts, and a third membership. Only 25% would stay after the offer; 19% chose Ownly Plus and 3 of 8 of them placed an order.

Watch two numbers every month: second orders after the offer ends, and orders that go wrong. If second orders don't come, stop the discounts rather than doubling them.

Evidence: A-fee-ownly, MM1-2, T3, F4-not_he, F6, F7, V-App-st-Suppor, K7, F8-membership - see the last page for each calculation.

# Outreach Scripts — copy, paste, send (v3)

**Owner: Person 2 (interviews) · all three (survey distribution).**
**Nothing goes out before written ethics approval.** Every message below states that this is student
research, that it is anonymous, and that there is no payment. Do not edit those three facts out to
make a message shorter.

## Ground rules
- **Ask a group admin before posting in any group you don't own.** Screenshot the permission.
- One post per group. No re-posting, no DM blasting the same person twice.
- Never imply you are from Ownly, Rapido, Swiggy or Zomato, and never say "survey by Ownly".
- Do not offer money, food or credit. We have no incentive budget and a promised incentive we can't pay is an ethics problem.

---

## A · Interview ask — someone you know (WhatsApp DM)
> Hey! I'm doing a college research project on how people our age pick food-delivery apps. Could I ask you about it for 15 minutes — call or in person, whenever suits you? No prep needed, I just want to hear about your actual last few orders. It's anonymous, nothing recorded, and it's coursework, not for any company. Free sometime today or tomorrow?

## B · Interview ask — a friend-of-a-friend (referred)
> Hi [name] — [referrer] passed on your number, hope that's okay. I'm a student at [university] doing a project on food-delivery habits around Gachibowli. Would you have 15 minutes sometime today or tomorrow for a few questions about how you order? Anonymous, nothing recorded, purely a course project. Totally fine to say no!

## C · Interview ask — a colleague at work (Teams / Slack / WhatsApp)
> Hi! Quick ask unrelated to work — I'm doing a research project for my course on how people around Financial District / Gachibowli choose food-delivery apps. Looking for 15 minutes with people who order fairly regularly. Anonymous, no recording, no company involved. Would you be up for it this evening or tomorrow?

## D · Interview ask — someone in your hostel / PG / apartment
> Hi! I'm doing a college project on food delivery around here. Can I grab 15 minutes to ask about how you order — what you ordered last, what annoys you, that kind of thing? Anonymous and nothing recorded. I'll come to you.

## E · The no-show / no-reply follow-up — send **once**, then stop
> No worries if you're busy! If 15 minutes doesn't work, this 7-minute anonymous survey helps just as much: [link] 🙏

## I · Survey — the personal nudge that actually works
Send this **individually** to 15 people each. One-to-one messages convert several times better than
any group post, and they are the reason we will or won't hit the sample.
> Hey, could you do me a favour? 7-minute anonymous survey for my course project on food delivery — takes less time than picking a restaurant 😄 [link] — and if you could forward it to one other person who orders a lot, that'd be huge.

## J · Group-admin permission request

It collects no personal data and isn't connected to any company. Would that be okay? Happy to send you the survey first so you can see it.

## Where to actually recruit — ranked by yield per hour

| # | Source | Why it works | Target | Screen for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your own classmates and course cohort** | Instant, in-catchment, right age | 25–30 responses | Nothing — they qualify |
| 2 | **Hostel / PG / co-living WhatsApp groups** | Dense, high order frequency, exactly the segment Ownly names | 20–30 | Admin permission first |
| 3 | **Individual DMs (script I)** | Highest conversion of anything on this list | 45 sent → ~25 back | — |
| 4 | **Office / intern colleagues in Gachibowli, Financial District, HITEC City** | The *working professional* half of the quota, which is always the harder half | 20–25 | Ask early — it's the bottleneck |
| 5 | **Apartment / society groups (Manikonda, Narsingi, Kondapur, Tellapur)** | Older, higher basket, out of the student bubble | 10–15 | Admin permission |
| 6 | **Alumni and seniors now working in Hyderabad** | Professionals, easy warm ask | 10 | — |
| 7 | **LinkedIn (script G)** | Reaches professionals we have no group access to | 10–15 | Note `meta_found_survey` |
| 8 | **Friends in Bengaluru** | The **benchmark** sample — 15–30 is enough | 15–30 | Ask specifically "do you order food in Bengaluru?" |
| 9 | **Known Rapido users** | The distribution hypothesis needs them | 10+ | Ask "do you use Rapido?" before sending |

> **The two quotas that will slip if you don't watch them: working professionals, and Bengaluru.**
> Students will over-fill on their own. Check the split at every progress report and redirect effort.
> Do **not** let IIIT-H (or any single campus) exceed ~40% of the Hyderabad sample — record
> `meta_found_survey` and watch it.

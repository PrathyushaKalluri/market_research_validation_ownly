# Interview Consent and Privacy Notice

**Version:** v2, 2026-09-14. Read aloud (or share in writing) **before** any recording starts. Log consent in `interview_log_template.csv` (`consent_logged` = verbal_recorded / written / declined_recording).

> Fill every `[bracket]` before use. If your university has an ethics/IRB process for student research, follow it. This template does not replace it.

## A. Participant information (plain language)

**Who we are.** We are [names or "a team of 3 students"] from [university/programme], doing a course research project on how people aged 20–30 in Hyderabad decide how and where to order food.

**Independence.** This is independent academic research. It is **not** run by, paid for, or done on behalf of Rapido, Ownly, Swiggy, Zomato, Magicpin or any restaurant or delivery company. We may mention some of these brands during the conversation. That is not an endorsement, and you can say whatever you honestly think.

**What you'll be asked.** We'll ask about real, recent times you ordered (or decided not to order) food, what happened, and why. There are no right or wrong answers, and we're not testing you. Near the end we may show a short description of a food-delivery idea and ask what you think.

**Time.** About [30 / 45] minutes. You can stop at any time.

**Voluntary.** Taking part is your choice. You can skip any question, pause, or stop, with no need to give a reason. [If there is an incentive: You receive the ₹[__] voucher even if you stop early or skip questions.]

**Recording.** With your permission, we'll **audio-record** (video off, or video not saved) so our notes are accurate. If you prefer not to be recorded, we'll take written notes only. Either is fine.

**Order history ("show me").** We might ask whether you'd like to open a food app on your phone and walk us through a recent order. This is **completely optional**. If you do, please scroll past or cover your name, phone number, address, and payment details. We won't photograph or screenshot your screen. We'll only note things like the price breakdown and time.

**What we do with the information.**
- Recordings are transcribed, then personal details (names, phone numbers, exact addresses, employer names, college names where identifying, names of friends) are **removed or replaced** (e.g. "[my company]", "[friend]").
- You are identified only by a code such as `HYD-07`. Your contact details are stored separately from what you said, and they are not linked in any shared file.
- Short **anonymous quotes** may appear in our report, dashboard or presentation, described only in general terms (e.g. "working professional, Financial District, frequent orderer"). We will not use a quote that could identify you.
- We will not share your recording with any company, including the companies named above.

**Storage and retention.** Recordings are kept in a private folder that only the research team can access, and are **deleted within [30 days] after the project is graded / by [date]**. Anonymised transcripts and notes may be kept until [date] for academic assessment. Contact details are deleted once the incentive is sent and the project ends.

**Withdrawing.** You can withdraw up to [7 days] after the interview by messaging [contact]. We'll then delete your recording, transcript and notes. After that, your anonymised input may already be combined into the analysis.

**Questions or concerns.** Contact [team member, email/WhatsApp] or [course instructor/supervisor name, email].

## B. Consent checklist (interviewer reads and records each answer)

Say this at the start of the recording (if the person agreed to recording), or note the answers in writing:

1. "Have you read or heard the information about this research, and do you have any questions?" ☐ Yes, understood
2. "Do you agree to take part in this conversation?" ☐ Yes ☐ No → end politely
3. "Do you agree to us audio-recording it for note accuracy?" ☐ Yes ☐ No (notes only)
4. "Are you OK with us using short anonymous quotes from what you say in our report and presentation?" ☐ Yes ☐ No
5. "Near the end we might ask you, if you want, to show a recent order in your app. You can say no then. OK to ask?" ☐ Yes ☐ No
6. "Can we contact you once in the next 2 weeks if we need to clarify something you said?" ☐ Yes ☐ No

Recording opener (only after 1–3 are yes): *"Today is [date], this is interview [HYD-__]. Can you confirm you're happy for this to be recorded?"*

## C. Written consent version (for in-person or Google Form)

☐ I agree to take part. ☐ I agree to audio recording. ☐ I agree to anonymous quotes being used.
Participant code: ____ Date: ____ (No signature or name is needed on the analysis copy.)"

## D. Interviewer data-handling rules
- Never write the participant's name in notes, transcripts or file names. Use `HYD-##` / `BLR-##` only.
- File names: `HYD-07_2026-09-18_audio.m4a`, `HYD-07_transcript_redacted.docx`.
- Redact during transcription: names, phone numbers, exact addresses/flat/PG names, employer names, specific college names when combined with other details, delivery partner names, order IDs.
- If an AI transcription or coding tool is used, upload **only redacted** transcripts or audio **with no identifying details**, and check the tool's data-retention settings first. Tell participants if a third-party transcription tool will process the audio (add a line in Section A if so).
- Do not photograph or screenshot a participant's phone during the "show me" section. Write down the fee breakdown numbers only.
- If the participant describes a safety incident (e.g. harassment by a delivery partner), do not probe for details beyond what they want to share. Offer to pause, and do not record identifying details of third parties.

**What we collect → why → how → analysis → decision**

| What | Why | How | Analysis | Decision it enables |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14–16 behavioural interviews in Hyderabad (Gachibowli and nearby) + optional 5–6 Bengaluru Ownly-user interviews | Surveys show *how many* and *how much*. ## 1. Hyderabad quota grid (target 16 completed; minimum defensible 12)

Primary cells: occupation × ordering frequency (orders in the last 4 weeks: occasional 1–3, regular 4–7, frequent 8+).

| Cell | Students (`student`, incl. `working_student` if mainly studying) | Working professionals (`working_professional`) | Row total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frequent (8+ orders / 4 wk) | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| Regular (4–7) | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| Occasional (1–3) | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| **Column total** | **8** | **8** | **16** |

### Overlay quotas (fill these *inside* the grid above; one person can meet several)

| Overlay quota | Minimum | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Current or lapsed **Ownly Hyderabad users** (placed ≥1 Ownly order in Hyderabad) | 3 (aim for ≥1 lapsed: tried, then stopped) | H9, H12: early transfer evidence and repeat/churn stories |
| **Heavy Swiggy/Zomato loyalists**: frequent users *and* (active Swiggy One/Zomato Gold subscription *or* one app used for ≥80% of orders) | 3 | Voices likely to disconfirm. Stops the sample from favouring people predisposed to switch. |
| **Non-IIIT-H students** (e.g. University of Hyderabad, ISB, other colleges near Gachibowli/Kondapur) | ≥4 of the 8 students | IIIT-H is one channel, not a proxy for students (standing rule) |
| Students by **living arrangement**: hostel vs PG/shared flat | ≥2 of each | Campus hostels may have delivery gate limits, mess food and different fees. The job-to-be-done likely differs. |
| Professionals by **workplace cluster**: Gachibowli / Financial District–Nanakramguda / HITEC City–Madhapur / Kondapur | ≥1 per cluster, max 3 from one cluster | Geographic spread. Office-delivery vs home-delivery occasions differ. |
| Professionals by **living**: alone/shared flat vs with family | ≥2 living with family *if* they order ≥1×/4 wk | Stops the professional sample from being all single flat-sharers |
| Gender | Neither gender >70% of completes | Basic balance. Safety and delivery-handoff concerns may differ. |
| **Unaware of Ownly** | ≥4 | Brand-blind reactions that the brand has not already coloured |

**Disallowed in the Hyderabad sample:** anyone outside ages 20–30; anyone not ordering food delivery in the last 4 weeks; screener disqualifiers (see `screener.md`).

### Minimum defensible fallback (if recruitment stalls by the mid-point check)
12 completes: 6 students + 6 professionals; ≥4 frequent; ≥2 Ownly users (current or lapsed); ≥2 heavy loyalists. If we fall short of this, label every qualitative finding **"exploratory — below planned saturation"** in the dashboard and deck.

### Saturation check
After interview 10, list new codes per interview. If interviews 11–13 add **no new first-level code relevant to H1–H12**, the team may stop at 13–14. Record the saturation table in `coding_framework.md` §8. Do not stop before filling the minimum Ownly-user and loyalist overlays.

## 2. Optional Bengaluru Ownly-user module (5–6 interviews) — for H9 and H12

Run this only if Bengaluru Ownly users can be reached. It is the only direct "why did it work there" evidence.

| Cell | Target |
|---|---|
| Active Ownly users (≥2 Ownly orders in the last 4 weeks) | 3 |
| Lapsed Ownly users (ordered ≥1× before, none in the last 6 weeks) | 2 |
| Student vs professional | ≥2 of each across the module |

Eligibility: ages 20–30, lives/works in Bengaluru, **self-reports an Ownly order in the last 3 months** (active) or earlier (lapsed), and can show the order in app history (optional, redacted) or describe it specifically. Use the Ownly-user sections of the 45-minute guide, drop the Hyderabad brand-blind concept test, and add the Bengaluru-context probes marked `[BLR]`.

## 3. Recruitment channels (in priority order)

| # | Channel | Target cells | First action | Owner (role) | Notes / guardrails |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hyderabad survey opt-in** (separate, unlinked interview-interest form shown at the end of the survey) | All, especially Ownly users and loyalists (screen on survey answers they re-enter in the screener) | Add the link to the survey thank-you page | Person 2 | Best source for quota precision. Keep contact details in a **separate form** so survey responses stay anonymous. |
| 2 | Student networks: IIIT-H + University of Hyderabad + ISB + other colleges near Gachibowli (club WhatsApp groups, hostel groups, student Reddit/Discord) | Students | Post the screener link with a neutral message (below) | Person 2 | Cap IIIT-H at 4 of 8 students |
| 3 | LinkedIn: people located in Hyderabad at companies in the Financial District / HITEC City / Gachibowli (2nd-degree connections, alumni) | Professionals | DM with the screener link (no brand mention) | Person 3 | Cap at 2 per employer |
| 4 | Gachibowli / Kondapur apartment, PG and flat-hunt groups (Facebook, Telegram, Reddit r/hyderabad) | Professionals, PG students | Post only where group rules allow research requests | Person 2/3 | Follow group rules; one post per group |
| 5 | Co-working spaces / office cafeterias (intercept with QR code for the screener) | Professionals | Ask the space manager for permission first | Person 3 | Never recruit at restaurants or delivery pickup points (sample bias + trespass) |
| 6 | Ownly-user targeting: Reddit/X threads where Hyderabad users mention Ownly (public), friends-of-friends | Ownly users | Public reply or DM **only where the platform allows it**, neutral wording | Person 1 | Do not say "we want Ownly fans". Say "people who've tried different food apps recently, including newer ones". |
| 7 | Snowball (ask each interviewee for one referral **in an under-filled cell**) | Gaps | End-of-interview ask | Interviewer | Max 2 referrals per seed so one social circle doesn't dominate |
| 8 | Bengaluru module: Bengaluru friends/alumni networks, r/bangalore, LinkedIn Bengaluru | BLR Ownly users | Screener with a Bengaluru branch | Person 1 | Same neutral wording |

**Neutral recruitment message (Hyderabad):**
> "Hi! We're a student research team studying how people in and around Gachibowli decide where and how to order food delivery. It's independent research (not run by or for any food-delivery company). If you're 20–30 and have ordered food delivery in the last 4 weeks, you might be eligible for a 30–45 min conversation [incentive, if any]. Short screener (2 min): [link]"

Do **not** mention Ownly, Rapido, "cheaper", "hidden fees" or "new app" in recruitment material. It self-selects price-pain respondents and primes the brand-blind section.

## 4. Incentive suggestion
- ₹150–₹250 digital voucher per completed interview (for example a generic e-gift card **not tied to any food-delivery platform**, to avoid priming or rewarding a specific platform). Give the same amount for 30- and 45-minute sessions if money is short. If you pay more for 45 minutes, record it.
- If there is no budget: offer a short summary of the anonymised findings, plus thanks. Record `incentive_given` in the log so we can check later whether incentivised and non-incentivised interviewees differ.
- Never make the incentive depend on what a person says or on liking any concept.

## 5. Over-recruit, scheduling and no-show handling
- **Over-recruit by 30%**: book ~21 confirmed slots to land 16 completes (student no-show rates are typically high; this is an ASSUMPTION, so record the actual rate in the log).
- Confirm the day before and again 2 hours before (WhatsApp/email), with the time and a join link or place.
- **No-show rule:** wait 10 minutes. Send one reschedule offer. If they miss the second slot, mark the row `no_show` and open a replacement in the **same quota cell** (do not backfill with whichever cell is easiest).
- **Quota tracking:** update the `interview_log_template.csv` after every session. At the mid-point (after 8 completes), check the grid; if any cell is at <50% of target, move all channel effort to that cell.
- **Scheduling windows:** students 17:00–21:00 weekdays or weekends. Professionals 13:00–14:00 (lunch), 19:30–21:30, or weekends. Avoid scheduling during their own dinner-ordering time *unless* doing a live "show me" session they agreed to.
- **Interviewer pairing:** 1 interviewer + 1 note-taker where possible. Rotate interviewers across segments so interviewer and segment are not confounded (for example, do not let one person interview only students).

## 6. Recruitment ethics
- Adults only (20–30). Voluntary. Consent is logged before recording (`consent_and_privacy.md`).
- Do not recruit employees of Rapido/Ownly, Swiggy, Zomato, Magicpin, other delivery/quick-commerce firms, restaurant owners/staff, or market-research professionals (screener disqualifiers).
- Store contact details only in the recruitment sheet, which is kept separate from transcripts. Delete them after the incentive is paid and the project ends (see the retention rule in the consent file).

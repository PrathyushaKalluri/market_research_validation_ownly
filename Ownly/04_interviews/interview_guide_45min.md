# Interview Guide — 45-minute version (Hyderabad / Gachibowli; Ownly-user and Bengaluru branches)

**Version:** v2, 2026-09-14. Replaces root `14_ownly_research_design.md` §6–7.
**Method:** behavioural, critical-incident interviewing. We reconstruct **specific past episodes** ("the last time…") and probe what happened, why, and what came next. We do **not** ask people to predict their own future behaviour, except in the clearly separated concept section at the end, and we treat those answers as weak evidence.

### What changed from v1 and why
| v1 problem | v2 fix |
|---|---|
| Ownly concept + "₹30 delivery" introduced mid-interview → primed everything after it | Unaided awareness is captured early. Any concept appears only in the **last section**: first a **brand-blind** card, then brand reveal. No price figure on the card. |
| "How much would you need to save to switch?" (hypothetical, anchors on price) | Replaced by **past switching episodes** and **past trade-off incidents** |
| "What frustrates you?" (invites generic complaints) | Replaced by "last time the final bill felt unreasonable" / "last abandoned order", with frequency probes |
| Price-led ordering of topics | Order-reconstruction first; price, ETA, selection, reliability and support each raised only if the participant raises it or through incident prompts covering all of them equally |

## 1. Consent + warm-up (4 min)
Read the consent script (`consent_and_privacy.md` §B). Start recording only after consent.

> "There are no right or wrong answers. We're interested in what actually happened, not what should happen. If you don't remember something, 'I don't remember' is a perfectly good answer."

**Q1.1 [M]** "To start, tell me a little about a normal weekday for you: where you are around lunch and dinner time, and who you usually eat with."
- [O] "How is a weekend different?"
- Listen for: hostel mess / office cafeteria / cooking at home / roommates. These are the **alternatives to delivery**.

**Q1.2 [M]** "In a typical week, what are the main ways you get meals? Cooking, mess or canteen, eating out, ordering in, something else?"
- [M] "Roughly how many of those are delivery?" (Check against the screener answer; note any difference.)

---

## 2. Last dinner-order reconstruction (8 min)
**Q2.1 [M]** "Think about the **last time you ordered dinner** for delivery. Do you remember roughly when that was?"
*(If they can't remember dinner, use the last delivery order of any kind and note `occasion_substituted`.)*

**Q2.2 [M]** "Take me back to that moment. **What happened from the moment you decided not to cook or go out?**"
Let them tell it. Then walk the timeline:
- [M] "What made you decide to order rather than [cook / go to the mess / go out]?"
- [M] "Who was it for: just you, or others too?"
- [M] "**Which app or option did you open first?** Why that one?"
- [M] "Did you already know what you wanted, or did you browse? How did you pick the restaurant?"
- [M] "Did you look at anything else before ordering: another app, another restaurant, calling the restaurant?"
- [M] "When you got to the checkout, what do you remember about the total?" → "Did anything there make you pause or change the order?"
- [O] "Did you use a coupon or offer? How did you find it? Would you have ordered without it?"
- [M] "What time did it arrive, compared with what the app said? What did you do while waiting?"
- [M] "How was the food and the delivery when it came?"
- [M] "**Looking back, was that order worth it?** Why / why not?"
- [O] "Is that a typical order for you? What's different on other days?"

**Probe discipline (use throughout):** *Why? → What happened next? → How often does that happen? → What did you do instead? → How much did that matter to you?*

---

## 3. "Show me" order-history walkthrough (4 min, OPTIONAL)
Only if they agreed in consent §B.5. Remind them: *"Please hide your name, address, phone number and payment details. We won't screenshot."*

**Q3.1** "Could you open the app you used most recently and go to your past orders? Pick any recent order and walk me through it."
- "Can you open the bill details? Read out the line items, whatever is shown." → note item total, packaging, platform fee, delivery fee, other fees, taxes, discount, final amount. **Record numbers only.**
- "Looking at this now, is there anything you hadn't noticed before?" *(observe; don't lead)*
- "Scroll through the last few orders. Are there patterns? Same restaurants, same time, same app?"
- [O] If they use two apps: "Can you show the most recent order in the other app too?"

Note in the template: `show_me_done` = yes/no and the fee lines observed (tag them **OBSERVATION**, because they were seen, not recalled).

---

## 4. App choice, multi-homing, subscriptions, unaided awareness (5 min)
**Q4.1 [M]** "Which food apps or ways of ordering do you have on your phone right now?"
**Q4.2 [M]** "When do you use each one? Tell me about the last time you used the **less-used** one. What made you open it that time?"
**Q4.3 [M]** "Do you have any membership like Swiggy One or Zomato Gold, or have you had one before?"
- If yes [M]: "What made you get it? Has it changed how often or where you order? The last time you checked whether it was worth it, what did you find?"
- If lapsed [O]: "What made you stop?"
**Q4.4 [M] (unaided awareness; do not name any brand)** "Apart from the ones you use, **which other food-ordering apps or options have you heard of**, even ones you've never tried?"
- For each named: [M] "Where did you hear about it?" [O] "Have you tried it? What happened?"
- **Record verbatim whether Ownly/Rapido is mentioned unprompted** → `ownly_unaided` = yes/no. **Do not prompt.**

---

## 5. Critical incidents (10 min: cover ≥3 incident types; prioritise those not yet raised)
For each incident: *When? What happened? What did you do? What happened next? How often? How much did it matter? Did it change what you did afterwards?*

**5A — Final bill [M]** "**When did you last feel the final food bill was unreasonable?**" *(If "never": "What's the closest you've come to thinking a bill was too much?")*
- [M] "What exactly felt unreasonable: the food prices, a specific charge, the total?"
- [M] "What did you do? Order anyway, change the order, switch app, cancel?"
- [O] "Have you ever compared the app price with the price at the restaurant itself? What happened?" *(Only if they raise menu price differences; otherwise ask neutrally: "Have you ever compared prices across apps or with the restaurant?")*

**5B — Abandonment [M]** "**When have you last started an order and not completed it?**"
- [M] "At which point did you stop? What made you stop?" (price / fees / ETA / restaurant closed or missing / minimum order / payment / changed mind / someone else ordered)
- [M] "What did you eat instead?"
- [O] "How often does that happen in a month?"

**5C — Late / cancelled / wrong / refund [M]** "Tell me about the **last time an order went wrong**: late, cancelled, wrong item, spilled, missing items."
- [M] "What did you do? Did you contact support? How did it go?"
- [M] "Did you get a refund or credit? How long did it take? Was it enough?"
- [M] "Did it change which app or restaurant you used next time?"

**5D — Missing restaurant [O→M if not yet covered]** "Has there been a time when a restaurant you wanted **wasn't available** on the app you opened?"
- "What did you do? Switch app, choose another restaurant, go out, skip?"

**5E — Cheaper option disappointed [M]** "**When has a cheaper option disappointed you?** A cheaper restaurant, app, or deal."
- [M] "What made you pick it? What went wrong? Did you go back?"

**5F — Waiting [O]** "Think of a time the delivery time shown made you change your plan. What did the app say, and what did you do?"

## 6. Past switching and trade-off episodes (5 min)
**Q6.1 [M] Switching** "Has there ever been a time you **moved most of your ordering from one app or option to another**, or added a new one? Tell me about that."
- [M] "What was going on at the time? What finally made you do it?" (**push**)
- [M] "What did the new one offer?" (**pull**)
- [M] "Was there anything that made you hesitate?" (**anxiety**)
- [M] "What made it hard to leave the old one?" (**habit**)
- If never switched [M]: "What has kept you with the same app?" → "Has anything almost made you switch? What happened?"

**Q6.2 [M] Trade-off** "Have you ever **waited longer, or picked a less-preferred restaurant, to pay less**?"
- [M] "What was the saving roughly, and what did you give up? How did that turn out? Would you do the same again? Why?"
- [M] Reverse: "And the opposite: a time you **paid more to get it faster, more reliably, or from a specific place**? What happened?"
- Listen for rough ₹ ↔ minutes ↔ reliability numbers **from real episodes**. Write them down as recalled (mark "recalled estimate").

---

## 7a. Branch O — current or lapsed Ownly users (5 min, only if the screener says Ownly user)
Ask only after §4.4 (unaided awareness) is complete. If they have already mentioned Ownly, continue from their account; if not, bridge neutrally: *"In the form you mentioned you've used Ownly. Tell me about that."*

- **O1 [M] First trial:** "Tell me about the **first time** you ordered on Ownly. Where did you first come across it? What made you try it that day? What did you order?"
- **O2 [M] Last order:** "Walk me through your **most recent** Ownly order, start to finish. How did the total, the time and the food compare with what you'd expected?"
- **O3 [M] Chose competitor instead:** "Tell me about the **last time you chose Swiggy/Zomato (or another option) instead of Ownly**. What was different about that occasion?"
- **O4 [M] Issues:** "Has anything gone wrong with an Ownly order? What did you do, and how was it resolved?"
- **O5 [M] Frequency change:** "Since your first order, have you been using it more, less or about the same? What's driving that?"
- **O6 [M] Stop:** "Has there been a moment you almost stopped using it, or did stop? What happened?" *(for current users: "What has kept you using it?")*
- **O7 [O] Rapido link:** "Did the fact it's connected to Rapido matter at any point, positively or negatively? When?"
- **O8 [O] Restaurants:** "Were the restaurants you wanted on it? What did you do when one wasn't?"

**Branch O participants still do §8**, but skip the brand-blind card (they already know the proposition). Go straight to reveal-style probes O7 and "how would you describe Ownly to a friend in one sentence?" → `ownly_description_verbatim`.

## 7b. Branch B — Bengaluru Ownly-user module (replaces §8 for BLR participants)
Use O1–O8 plus:
- **B1 [M]** "Where in Bengaluru do you usually order to? Did coverage or ETA differ by area?"
- **B2 [M]** "When you first tried it, how many people around you already used it? How did you hear about it?"
- **B3 [M]** "What does Ownly do differently from the apps you'd used before, based on an actual order?"
- **B4 [O]** "Has anything about it got better or worse over the months you've used it? Give me an example."
- **B5 [O]** "Have you recommended it, or told someone not to use it? What did you say?" *(record what was said, not an NPS score)*

## 8. Concept section: brand-blind → brand reveal (6 min, Hyderabad non-Ownly users)
**Rules:** read the card exactly. Do not add prices. Do not say "cheaper". Show the card, stop talking, wait ≥5 seconds.

### 8.1 Brand-blind card (show; "Service X")
> **Service X** is a food-delivery app where the food prices shown are the restaurant's own prices, and the checkout shows a single delivery charge instead of separate platform, packaging or other fees. Restaurants on it do not pay the app a commission on each order. The delivery time and the number of restaurants available vary by area.

*(This card deliberately mentions a limitation, "delivery time and number of restaurants vary", so the concept does not read as a pure benefit, and it gives no ₹ figure.)*

- **8.1a [M]** "In your own words, **what is this offering?**" *(comprehension; note misunderstandings)*
- **8.1b [M]** "Think about the dinner order you told me about earlier. **If Service X had been on your phone that day, what do you think would have happened?**" *(anchor to the real episode)*
- **8.1c [M]** "What would you want to check or know **before** you ordered on it?" (probe: restaurants, time, support, trust, total)
- **8.1d [M]** "What could make this **not work** for you?"
- **8.1e [O]** "Who do you know that this would suit best? Who would it not suit?"
- **8.1f [M]** "On a scale from 'I'd never use it' to 'I'd use it for most orders', where would you honestly put yourself, and **why not higher?**" *(record the verbal placement; treat as stated intent = weak evidence)*

### 8.2 Brand reveal
> "Service X is actually **Ownly**, a food-delivery service from **Rapido**, the bike-taxi and auto company."

- **8.2a [M]** "Does knowing it's from Rapido change anything for you? In what way?" → probe **why**, and ask for a specific experience with Rapido (ride, captain, support) that shapes the answer.
- **8.2b [M]** "Does it change what you'd expect about delivery reliability? About support if something goes wrong? About trust with payment or food handling?" *(ask each separately; do not bundle)*
- **8.2c [M]** "Had you heard of Ownly before today?" (only now, **aided** awareness → `ownly_aided`) → "Where? What did you hear?"
- **8.2d [O]** "Has anyone you know used it? What did they say?"
- **8.2e [M]** "Where would you now place yourself on that same scale? **What made it move / not move?**"

**Do not ask:** "Would you use it if it were cheaper?", "Do you like the idea of no hidden fees?", "How much cheaper would it need to be?"

## 9. Close + referral (3 min)
- **9.1 [M]** "Of everything we talked about, **which one problem with ordering food matters most to you**, based on what has actually happened to you?"
- **9.2 [M]** "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"
- **9.3 [M]** Debrief: "Thank you. To be transparent: our project looks at whether food-delivery ideas that work in Bengaluru work the same way in Hyderabad, including newer services like Ownly. That's why we asked about it at the end and didn't mention it earlier: we didn't want to influence your earlier answers."
- **9.4 [O]** Referral (only for under-filled quota cells): "Do you know someone who [cell description, e.g. works in the Financial District and orders maybe once a week] who might be willing to talk to us?"
- Confirm incentive delivery. Stop recording.

**Immediately after (10 min, interviewer + note-taker):** complete the "Top 3 observations" and "surprises / contradictions" boxes in the note template while memory is fresh.

## Interviewer DO / DON'T

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|
| Ask about **specific past episodes** ("the last time…") | Ask "Do you usually…?" or "Would you…?" as a main question |
| Use their words back to them ("You said it felt 'too much'. What made it too much?") | Introduce words like "hidden fees", "markup", "rip-off", "cheap" before they do |
| Stay silent for 3–5 seconds after answers | Fill silences or suggest answer options |
| Ask for **frequency and consequence** ("How often? What did you do next?") | Accept a complaint without asking whether it changed behaviour |
| Probe contradictions gently ("Earlier you mentioned X, and now Y. Help me understand.") | Point out that they are being inconsistent |
| Explore the **positives of incumbents** as seriously as the pains | Treat a Swiggy/Zomato loyalist as a "failed" interview |
| Record "I don't remember" as-is | Push until a guess sounds like a memory |
| Keep the brand-blind → reveal order fixed | Show the Ownly name, logo or Rapido link before §8.2 |
| Note body language/hesitation as *observation* | Write your interpretation into the quote column |
| Stay neutral about all brands | Share your own opinions of any app |

## Bias traps to watch (and the countermeasure)
| Trap | Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| **Acquiescence / politeness** (saying yes to the concept to please the interviewer) | Independence statement; 8.1d "what could make this not work"; ask "why not higher?" |
| **Hypothetical-intent inflation** | The concept test comes last and is anchored to their real episode (8.1b). Stated intent is coded as weak evidence. |
| **Price-priming** | No price terms or ₹ figures on the card. Incident prompts cover ETA, reliability, selection and support equally. |
| **Recall bias / rosy retrospection** | Use "show me" where agreed. Ask for the most recent episode, not "a typical one". |
| **Leading follow-ups** | Use only the probe-discipline stems |
| **Interviewer confirmation bias** | Rotate interviewers across segments. Every debrief lists ≥1 piece of evidence *against* the working thesis. |
| **Brand halo/horn from Rapido** | Measured separately after brand-blind. Ask for the specific Rapido experience behind the reaction. |
| **Social desirability** (e.g. "I don't care about money") | Ask about behaviour (abandonment, coupon use) rather than attitudes |

## Addendum (lead, 2026-09-14): probes added from Reddit/LinkedIn/app-review signals

Add these as **optional [O] probes** in the sections named. They come from `05_review_mining/voc_synthesis_pre_fieldwork.md` (N1–N4). Use them only when the participant's own story gives you an opening. Never introduce the topic as a premise.

- **§5 Critical incidents, "went wrong" incident:** "How was it resolved? How long did it take? Did that change which app you opened next time?" If late night: "What time was it? Has that happened before at that hour?"
- **§5 / §6, price checking:** "Before ordering, did you check the price anywhere else? What did you compare — the dish price, the fees, or the final amount after coupons?"
- **§6 Switching episode, payment:** "How did you pay? Is there any payment method you need (cash, a meal card, a particular card offer) that decides which app you use?"
- **§8 after the concept card (8.1d), price durability:** if the participant mentions price or savings, ask "How long do you think prices like that would last? What makes you think so?" Do not suggest that prices will rise.
- **§7a Ownly users, first order:** "Tell me about your very first Ownly order. How did it go? Did that first order affect whether you ordered again?"

The 30-minute guide adds only the first-order probe (Ownly users) and the payment probe.

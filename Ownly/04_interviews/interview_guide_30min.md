# Interview Guide — 30-minute version

**Version:** v2, 2026-09-14. A condensed form of `interview_guide_45min.md`. Use it when a participant has only 30 minutes. Section numbers, card wording and the brand-blind → reveal order are **identical**, so both versions can be coded with the same framework. Record `guide_version = 30` in the log.

**Cut rules:** no "show me" (§3) unless the participant offers. Critical incidents are limited to 3 types (bill, abandonment, went-wrong). Keep only the **[M]** probes listed here. For probe discipline, question wording, DO/DON'T and bias traps, see the 45-minute guide.

| § | Section | Min |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consent + warm-up | 3 |
| 2 | Last dinner order reconstruction | 6 |
| 4 | App choice + unaided awareness | 4 |
| 5 | Critical incidents (3) | 7 |
| 6 | One switching or trade-off episode | 3 |
| 7a/8 | Ownly-user branch **or** brand-blind → reveal | 5 |
| 9 | Close + debrief | 2 |

---

## 1. Consent + warm-up (3 min)
Consent script (`consent_and_privacy.md` §B). Then:
- **1.1** "Tell me a little about a normal weekday: where you are around lunch and dinner, and who you eat with."
- **1.2** "Roughly how many meals a week are delivery?"

## 2. Last dinner-order reconstruction (6 min)
- **2.1** "Think about the **last time you ordered dinner** for delivery. **What happened from the moment you decided not to cook or go out?**"
- Probes: "Which app did you open first, and why?" / "Did you look at anything else before ordering?" / "At checkout, what do you remember about the total? Did anything make you pause?" / "When did it arrive compared with what the app said?" / "Looking back, was it worth it? Why?"

## 4. App choice + unaided awareness (4 min)
- **4.1** "Which food apps or ways of ordering do you use? Tell me about the last time you used the less-used one. Why that time?"
- **4.2** "Any membership like Swiggy One or Zomato Gold? What made you get it, or not?"
- **4.3 (unaided; no brand prompts)** "Which other food-ordering apps have you heard of, even ones you've never tried? Where did you hear about them?" → record `ownly_unaided`.

## 5. Critical incidents: 3 types (7 min)
For each: *When? What happened? What did you do? How often? Did it change what you did next?*
- **5A** "When did you last feel the **final food bill was unreasonable**?"
- **5B** "When did you last **start an order and not finish it**? Where did you stop, and what did you eat instead?"
- **5C** "Tell me about the **last time an order went wrong**: late, cancelled, wrong or missing items. Did you contact support? How was it resolved?"
*(If a missing restaurant or a cheaper-option disappointment came up naturally, follow it instead of 5C and note the substitution.)*

## 6. One episode (3 min)
Ask **one**, choosing the one more relevant to what you've heard so far:
- **6.1 Switch:** "Has there been a time you moved most of your ordering to a different app, or added one? What finally made you do it, and what made it hard?"
- **6.2 Trade-off:** "Have you ever waited longer or picked a less-preferred restaurant to pay less, or paid more to get it faster or more reliably? What happened? Would you do it again?"

## 7a. Ownly users only (5 min, replaces §8)
- **O1** "Tell me about your **first** Ownly order: where you heard of it and what made you try it that day."
- **O3** "Tell me about the **last time you chose another app instead of Ownly**. What was different?"
- **O4/O6** "Has anything gone wrong with an Ownly order, or has there been a moment you almost stopped? What happened?"

## 8. Brand-blind → reveal (5 min, non-Ownly users)
Show the **identical card** from the 45-minute guide §8.1 ("Service X…"). Wait ≥5 seconds.
- **8.1a** "In your own words, what is this offering?"
- **8.1b** "If Service X had been on your phone for that dinner order, what do you think would have happened?"
- **8.1d** "What could make this **not work** for you?"
- **8.1f** "From 'I'd never use it' to 'I'd use it for most orders', where are you, and why not higher?"

Reveal: *"Service X is actually **Ownly**, a food-delivery service from **Rapido**."*
- **8.2a** "Does knowing it's from Rapido change anything? Is there a specific Rapido experience behind that?"
- **8.2c** "Had you heard of Ownly before today? Where?" → `ownly_aided`
- **8.2e** "Where are you now on that scale? What moved it, or didn't?"

## 9. Close (2 min)
- "Of everything we discussed, which one problem with ordering food matters most to you, based on what has actually happened?"
- Debrief (same wording as 45-minute §9.3). Incentive. Stop recording.
- Interviewer + note-taker: 5-minute debrief. Fill "Top 3 observations" and "contradictions".

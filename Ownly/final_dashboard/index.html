Membership Erosion Rate (custom) — 25.0%

baskets Ownly wins on list+fees but loses once the incumbent MEMBERSHIP benefit alone applies / matched baskets (no coupon involved)

Why it is a good indicator. Isolates how much of Ownly's edge a subscription alone removes, before any coupon. Good indicator because a membership is a STANDING benefit (it applies to every order), whereas a coupon is episodic - so this is the durable half of the erosion.

median incumbent non-food share of bill - median Ownly non-food share

Why it is a good indicator. Ownly's structural edge expressed as the share of the bill that is NOT food. Good indicator because it is the one advantage Ownly controls directly: it survives without subsidy, unlike a discount, and it is invisible to the customer unless stated - which links it to the communication problem.

04The ₹30 equation

₹30 buys time. ₹30 buys reliability. ₹30 cannot buy away your usual restaurants.

The same ₹30, three trades, same 46 respondents. 85% will wait 15 minutes longer and 67% will accept worse reliability — but only 33% will give up their restaurants.

What will Gachibowli users trade for ₹30?

Hyderabad respondents aged 20–35, n=46. Forced binary choice, ₹260 vs ₹230 held constant across all three items. SURVEY — STATED PREFERENCE. McNemar (paired, assortment vs speed): p=8e-06.

Reading the result

Why this is the most decision-relevant chart in the study

It isolates one variable

The rupee amount never changes. Only the thing being given up changes. So the differences are attributable to the attribute, not to price sensitivity.

It contradicts the project's own prior

The pre-registered prediction (PAP §6.7, P3) was that Ownly's ~17-minute ETA deficit would block adoption. It was falsified — 85% accept the slower option. Speed is not the constraint. Assortment is.

05Price-to-switch fit

The saving people need is small. The saving Ownly delivers is large — until an incumbent coupon lands.

Median stated requirement is ₹50 of recurring saving. The observed structural saving is ₹114. That is a comfortable fit. After coupons the observed saving is −₹28, and the fit disappears entirely.

Price-to-switch fit — the interpretation

On list price, Ownly's saving clears the stated switching bar of every respondent who named one. That is a genuine structural achievement, and it is why price can acquire.

A recommendation this evidence changed

The interviews point the lever somewhere else

What we measured

An 85% discovery gap inside Rapido. The obvious inference is “fix the in-app placement”.

But that inference was never tested — and the interviews suggest it is the wrong first move.

What the interviews say

Trial comes from a friend's recommendation, influencers, Instagram and YouTube reviews. The one documented interviewee heard about Ownly from her brother — not from the Rapido app.

Survey agrees: after price, the most-cited trial trigger was “a friend telling me it works well”.

So the higher-value experiment is a referral or creator-led trial mechanism, not a placement change.

Interview evidence: T-4, T-6, I-01.9 in 04_interviews/interview_findings_coded.md. NOT ESTABLISHED: no CAC is calculated and none can be; whether either lever raises trial is untested — both are experiments, not inferences.

Interviews resolve a contradiction the survey alone could not

Reliability: accepted at the point of choice, fatal after repetition

The contradiction

The survey says 67% will accept worse reliability for ₹30. The review record says fulfilment failure is 72% of coded app-store reviews. Both cannot be the headline.

What the interviews resolve

Interviews report that people leave an app after frequent bad orders. Users accept a reliability risk when choosing; they leave after it actually happens, repeatedly. Different decision moments — so both findings hold. Reliability is a churn driver, not a trial blocker.

Interview evidence: 04_interviews/interview_findings_coded.md (T-5). This is why reliability sits in the retention guardrail on tab 13, not in the acquisition hook.

10Why challengers fail

Ownly has escaped the mechanism that killed the others — and inherited two more.

Its price gap is structural rather than subsidy-funded, which is genuinely different in kind from Foodpanda. But it sells the same restaurants more slowly, and its revenue architecture is thinner than any incumbent's.

Five failure mechanisms, scored against our own evidence

M1RENTED DEMANDPARTIALLY ESCAPED
HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

Foodpanda: 200,000 → 5,000 daily orders (−97.5%) when discounts stopped. ONDC −29% after incentive cuts.

OWNLY EVIDENCE (ours)

Ownly's gap is structural (fee removal), not discount-funded: non-food load 5% vs 28%/18%. But 89% of our sample expects the low prices to rise anyway.

HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

Zomato told the CCI ~1% of its restaurants are exclusive — 99% already multi-home. Thrive died at 3% commission.

OWNLY EVIDENCE (ours)

Ownly's zero-commission wins supply the incumbents already have: 91% overlap, 1 exclusive restaurant observed.

HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

Eternal Q1FY27: “the same restaurants, similar or longer delivery times… there's no new use case being unlocked here.”

Our own audit confirms both premises: 91% assortment overlap and +17 min quoted ETA. Survey shows speed is tolerated, so the binding half is assortment.

M4RELIABILITY FLOORUNKNOWN

13GTM decision

Keep the fee architecture. Adapt the price claim. Stop paying for speed.

Ownly's advantage is real but it is being communicated as a comparison that incumbent coupons can falsify at will — against a sample where 83% hold a membership. The constraint respondents actually named was assortment, and speed was not a constraint at all.

KEEP / ADAPT / DEPRIORITISE

KEEP
Zero platform, packaging and surge fees

Observed: non-food load 5% vs 28% (Swiggy). This is the structural advantage and it survives without subsidy.

KEEP
Transparent, coupon-free everyday pricing

100% of stated switching thresholds are cleared by the observed ₹114 saving.

ADAPT
“Cheaper than Swiggy/Zomato” as the headline claim

50% of price wins reverse after coupons and 25% on the membership benefit alone. Against the 83% who hold memberships, the claim is often false at their checkout. Reframe around the everyday bill, not a comparison.

ADAPT
Restaurant breadth as the supply goal

91% overlap means breadth reproduces the incumbent catalogue. Shift the target to named local depth — the one thing ₹30 could not buy.

ADAPT
Rapido as a distribution channel

85% of Rapido users had not noticed food in the app. Access exists; discovery must be built and measured, not assumed.

DEPRIORITISE
Speed / ETA parity investment

85% accept 15 minutes slower for ₹30, so the pre-registered prediction that ETA blocks adoption was falsified. Spend the money elsewhere.

DEPRIORITISE
First-order discount depth

35% say they would continue once a ₹100 offer ends, and 89% expect prices to rise. Deeper intro discounts buy the trial the historical record says does not stick.

Each call is traced to a named observation in this deck. No call rests on the historical challenger record alone.

Executive conclusion

The GTM answer

WHO

Students and young professionals in the Gachibowli catchment who order 2+ times a month and do not hold an incumbent membership. In our sample that is a minority — 17% — and it is the only group who sees Ownly's list-price advantage at their checkout.

ACQUISITION HOOK

A smaller everyday bill on restaurants they already order from — not a cheaper-than-X comparison that a coupon can falsify.

PRICE REQUIREMENT

Median stated requirement ₹50 recurring. Observed structural saving ₹114. The level is sufficient; its durability is the open question.

SUPPLY REQUIREMENT

Depth in the specific restaurants respondents already use. 67% will not trade them for ₹30 — the strongest single constraint measured in this study.

CHANNEL

Rapido is access, not discovery: 85% gap. Treat in-app placement as an experiment with a measured trial outcome.

EXPERIENCE GUARDRAIL

Quoted ETA +17 min is tolerated. Fulfilment and refund failure — the dominant theme in first-hand accounts — is not measured in Hyderabad and is the guardrail to instrument first.

RETENTION

Unknown. Stated continuation after the offer ends is 34.8% and the trial-to-repeat intent gap is 43.5pp, but no behavioural retention was observed.

COMPETITIVE RESPONSE

Already visible. 50% of Ownly's price wins reversed on coupons in a single audit slot, and the incumbents hold roughly ₹15/order of platform-fee headroom to compress the gap further.

VIABILITY

Unresolved. Ownly charged ₹0 to the customer and takes 0% commission in every captured row. Revenue per order in Gachibowli was ₹0 observed. Burn per order is UNKNOWN and is not estimated here.

What to test next

  1. Run the fake-door test that was never run. It is the only instrument that yields a behavioural first-order conversion rate, which is what H₀ is actually about.
  2. Audit named local restaurants, not showcase chains. Every Ownly price we hold is a chain; the strategy the evidence points to depends on depth we have not measured.
  3. Place 3 test orders. Quoted ETA is not delivery time. The reliability guardrail is currently unmeasured in Hyderabad.
  4. Conduct the 8 interviews. The mechanism behind every number in this deck is unvalidated.
  5. Re-audit against a membership-holding account. 83% of the target holds one; the list-price view is not their reality.

Listed in order of decision value, not ease. Item 1 is the only one that would let a future version of this study answer H₀ as originally written.

Why the null is not the end of the analysis

The more informative decomposed finding

The two bundled propositions did not separate — but the attributes inside them separated sharply.

Holding ₹30 constant, 85% traded speed and 67% traded reliability, but only 33% traded their usual restaurants (McNemar p=8e-06). A bundled-concept test asks the wrong question of a 46-person sample; the attribute trades answer it with the same respondents.
